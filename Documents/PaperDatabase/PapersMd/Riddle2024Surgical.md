## 摘要
本文综述了外科模拟在过去25年的发展及其影响，追溯了从以患者为基础的培训和传统的活体/尸体实验室向视频训练器和综合模拟中心的转变，并讨论了成本、空间占用和对手术室时间的历史性争论。文章指出先前的研究表明模拟培训与手术表现的提升相关，并记录自2009年以来全球认证的模拟机构的快速扩张。本文将模拟及正在兴起的人工智能驱动方法视为现代外科教育与培训未来的核心。

# Surgical Simulation: Virtual Reality to Artificial Intelligence

**作者:** Elijah W. Riddle, MD, Divya Kewalramani, MD, Mayur Narayan, MD, MPH, MBA, Daniel B. Jones, MD, MS

## Abstract
This article surveys the evolution and impact of surgical simulation over the last 25 years, tracing the shift from patient-based learning and traditional vivarium/cadaver labs to video trainers and comprehensive simulation centers. It discusses historical debates about cost, space, and disruption to operating room time, notes that simulation-based training has been associated with improvements in operative performance in prior work, and documents the rapid global expansion of accredited simulation institutes since 2009. The piece frames simulation and emerging AI-enabled approaches as central to the future of contemporary surgical education and training.
