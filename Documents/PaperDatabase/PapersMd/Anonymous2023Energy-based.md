## 摘要
本文提出一种基于能量的触觉渲染框架，用于实时外科手术仿真，利用物理工具与虚拟组织之间的能量交换来产生真实的触觉反馈，涵盖按压、抓取和拉拽等交互。在狭窄空间等挑战性场景中，通过物理域与虚拟域之间的稳定能量交换和软体工具的虚拟耦合来确保稳定性。该框架通过将物理工具与虚拟网格和软组织模型耦合，实现实时渲染，能够提供合乎情理的变形和力反馈。本文作为预印本，尚未经过同行评审。

# Energy-based Haptic Rendering for Real-time Surgical Simulation

**作者:** Anonymous

## Abstract
This paper proposes an energy-based framework for haptic rendering in real-time surgical simulation. The approach models energy exchange between a physical tool and virtual tissue to generate realistic haptic feedback, capturing interactions such as pressing, grasping, and pulling. The method aims to maintain stability in challenging scenarios, including narrow spaces, by ensuring stable energy exchange between the physical and virtual domains through a virtual coupling of a soft body and surgical tool. The framework supports real-time rendering by coupling a physical tool with virtual meshes and soft-tissue models, enabling plausible deformation and force feedback. The work is presented as a preprint and has not yet undergone peer review.
