   For global reconstruction, we implement a center-crop mosaic
approach. The system prioritizes extracting the low-distortion
1.5 × 1.5 mm2 central region from the fine tactile images for spa-
tial arrangement, and subsequently inserts the coarse scanning
images to fill the remaining gaps. Through the seamless stitching
of these multiresolution image segments, a precise global profile
reconstruction is achieved.


    V. EXPERIMENTAL EVALUATION AND EX VIVO VALIDATION
   In this section, we conducted a series of experiments to charac-
