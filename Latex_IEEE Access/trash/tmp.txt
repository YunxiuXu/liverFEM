方法 (Methods) - 边界条件设置 (Boundary Conditions Setup)

To enforce realistic physiological boundary conditions within our Finite Element Method (FEM) framework and prevent unphysical rigid body motion, zero-displacement constraints were applied to node clusters corresponding to primary anatomical anchors. Specifically, these constraints mimic three critical attachment regions derived from standard anatomical maps:

The Porta Hepatis (Liver Hilum): Constraints were applied to the nodal cluster surrounding the entry point of the hepatic portal vein and hepatic artery proper. This represents the fixed root of the liver.

The Groove for Inferior Vena Vava (IVC): Nodes along the retroperitoneal canal, where the liver is firmly attached to the IVC, were constrained to simulate posterior wall anchoring.

The Bare Area (Area Nuda): Direct diaphragmatic adhesion was simulated by constraining a discrete band of nodes on the posterior-superior surface, bounded by the coronary ligaments.

This multi-point, surface-based constraint strategy ensures global position stability while allowing realistic local deformation during interactive palpation and large-displacement lifting tasks.

中文大意（供你写中文稿件参考）：

为了在我们的有限元方法 (FEM) 框架内加强真实的生理边界条件并防止非物理的刚体运动，我们将零位移约束（即完全固定）应用于对应于主要解剖锚点的节点群。具体而言，这些约束模拟了衍生自标准解剖图的三个关键附着区域：

肝门 (Porta Hepatis)：约束应用于肝门静脉和肝固有动脉进入点周围的节点群。这代表了肝脏的固定根部。

下腔静脉沟 (Groove for Inferior Vena Cava)：沿着腹膜后下腔静脉管（肝脏在此处与下腔静脉牢固附着）的节点被约束，以模拟后壁锚定。

肝裸区 (Bare Area)：通过约束由冠状韧带界定的后上表面上的离散节点带，模拟膈肌直接粘连。
这种基于表面的多点约束策略在确保整体位置稳定的同时，允许在交互式触诊和大位移提升任务期间进行真实的局部变形。
